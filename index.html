What you can do

ChaloFit is a simple step counter for everyday walking. See progress through the day and unlock chests as you hit walking milestones. Step totals and coin history stay on your phone.

Count daily steps

The app reads step counts from the device when you allow Physical activity.

See chest progress

Watch today’s total and the next walking milestone at a glance.

No account needed

Use the app without sign-up. History stays on the device.

How it works

ChaloFit uses Android’s physical activity permission only to count steps. If you deny the permission, the counter stays at zero and the rest of the app still opens. Chests are an in-app motivation layer. Coins are stored locally. This app does not send you money and does not open a bank or wallet account.

The app may show rewarded video and interstitial ads. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings. ChaloFit is built for walking, not for banking.

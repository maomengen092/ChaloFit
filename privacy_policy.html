ChaloFit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 August 2026 · Last updated: 28 August 2026

This policy explains how the ChaloFit mobile application (“App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. ChaloFit is a step-tracking utility with optional in-app coins and ads. The App does not create user accounts.

1. Who we are

ChaloFit is operated by the developer that publishes the App on Google Play. For privacy questions, contact support through the in-app Contact us link or email privacy@chalofit.club.

2. Information we process

We keep data collection to what the App needs to work:

  • Physical activity / step data. If you grant the Physical activity permission, the App reads step counts from the device to show today’s steps and unlock chests. This data is stored on your device. We do not upload a step history to our servers.
  • Local app state. Coins, chest counts, coin history, settings and similar values are stored only on the device.
  • Advertising ID and device data for ads. If ads are shown, the advertising ID plus basic device and app data (for example OS version, device type, country, language, IP address processed by ad partners) may be used by our mediation SDK and ad networks to request, serve and measure ads.
  • Install / campaign data. Play Install Referrer and Adjust may receive install source, campaign identifiers, device and app technical data (for example OS version, device type, country, language, advertising ID) to measure ads and app opens.
  • Diagnostics. Crash or performance logs may be collected in anonymized or aggregated form to keep the App stable.
  • Support messages. If you message support (for example via Telegram), that service processes whatever you send. We do not require a name or email to use the App.

We do not ask for contacts, SMS, precise location, photos, microphone, or payment card details. The App has no sign-up and no password.

3. How we use information

  • Count steps and operate chests, coins and local history
  • Show rewarded and interstitial ads
  • Measure installs, opens and ad events (including Adjust events such as app open and ad impressions)
  • Respond if you contact support
  • Comply with law and store policies

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use step data to build an advertising profile beyond what the ad and measurement SDKs do as described here.

4. Legal bases (if GDPR / similar laws apply)

Where those laws apply, we process data to provide the App you asked for (contract / requested service), with your permission for Physical activity and ads, and for legitimate interests in measuring campaigns and keeping the App secure. You may revoke permission in Android settings and turn off activity access or the advertising ID.

5. Sharing

Device-side step and coin data stay on the device unless you screenshot or copy them yourself. We share limited technical and advertising data with:

  • TopOn (ad mediation) — selects and loads ads. See TopOn Privacy Policy.
  • Pangle — ad network. See Pangle Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Ads — ad network. See Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Liftoff (Vungle) — ad network. See Vungle Privacy Policy.
  • InMobi — ad network. See InMobi Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play services — store, Play Install Referrer and related device services. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Adjust — attribution and in-app events (app open, ad impressions, support-link clicks). See Adjust Privacy Policy.
  • Telegram — only if you open Contact us and send a message. See Telegram’s own policy.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the App and users.

6. Retention

Local step, coin and history data remain on the device until you clear app data, uninstall the App, or use Clear local data in Settings. Advertising and measurement partners retain data under their own policies. We do not operate a cloud profile for you.

7. Security

We store App state on-device using standard Android storage. No method of storage is 100% secure. Keep your device locked and updated.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it where we control it.

9. International users

The App is intended for users in India. Advertising and measurement partners may process data in other countries, including where those partners operate servers.

10. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke Physical activity permission in Android settings. Steps will stay at 0.
  • Reset or delete advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Opt out of ads personalization where Google provides that control.
  • Clear local data in the App or uninstall the App.

Because there is no account, we cannot remotely “download all your data” from a server copy we do not keep.

11. Google Play Data safety summary

  • Health / physical activity: collected on device to run step features; not shared by us as a user profile
  • Device or other IDs: advertising ID for ads and Adjust
  • App activity: install source and in-app events for measurement
  • Data is processed by Google ads and Adjust as independent controllers / processors for their services

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
